    About Us

    About Us

    PACIFIC CORPORATION (Pramod Enterprises), established in 2006 in Mumbai, Maharashtra, is a renowned organization specializing in OHSE consultancy, civil construction services, and comprehensive turnkey projects. The company offers a diverse range of services, including electrical works, plumbing, civil construction, roads and industrial roads, interior design, and custom modular furniture solutions. Additionally, we are manufacturers and suppliers of high-quality office, home, and showroom furniture, as well as FRP ladders, all customized to meet the specific requirements of our esteemed clients. Our modern manufacturing facility, equipped with advanced machinery and technology, enables us to ensure the highest standards of quality and precision in every product and service we provide. PACIFIC CORPORATION is committed to delivering cost-effective, high-quality construction projects on schedule, while maintaining a focus on client satisfaction. Under the leadership of Mr. Pramod Jagannath, a civil engineering expert with over 26 years of experience, we uphold the principles of professionalism, integrity, and excellence in all our endeavors.
