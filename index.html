    About Us

    Reliable delivery for demanding facilities

    PACIFIC CORPORATION (Pramod Enterprises), established in 2006 in Mumbai, delivers OHSE consultancy, civil construction and turnkey project solutions for commercial and industrial clients.

    Our capabilities span electrical and plumbing works, roads, interiors, modular furniture, HVAC, laboratories, warehouses and custom workplace products. We combine practical engineering with careful coordination so projects are delivered safely, to specification and on schedule.
